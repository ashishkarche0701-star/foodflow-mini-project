## Introduction and problem statement
FoodFlow demonstrates a simple online food ordering workflow and the use of a FIFO queue to model pending orders.

## Objectives
- Display menu items and accept a customer order.
- Validate that a customer and at least one item are selected.
- Store order details and update order status.
- Demonstrate first-in, first-out ordering of pending work.

## Methodology
Python, Flask, SQLite, HTML and CSS. The Flask version stores orders in SQLite; pending order IDs are represented with `collections.deque` in FIFO order.
